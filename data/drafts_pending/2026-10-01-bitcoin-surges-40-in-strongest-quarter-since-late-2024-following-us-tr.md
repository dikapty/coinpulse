# Bitcoin Surges 40% in Strongest Quarter Since Late 2024 Following US Treasury Buybacks

Bitcoin has registered a major market rally, climbing 40% in what marks its best quarterly performance since late 2024. According to reporting by [Bitcoin Magazine](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/bitcoin-has-its-best-quarter-since-2024), the substantial price increase was largely driven by macroeconomic triggers, most notably the announcement of a debt buyback program by the United States Department of the Treasury.

The steep upward trajectory places the digital asset on course for one of its strongest historical quarters. The sharp rise highlights how deeply crypto asset valuations remain tied to broader sovereign fiscal policies and monetary adjustments. When public institutions engage in debt management strategies like buybacks, liquidity dynamics across global markets frequently shift, driving investor capital toward alternative assets including major cryptocurrencies.

## Treasury Debt Buyback Program Acts as Key Catalyst

The primary driver behind Bitcoin's sustained quarter-over-quarter growth was the US Treasury's decision to launch a debt buyback program. Capital markets reacted quickly to the announcement, with the majority of Bitcoin's 40% gains accumulating immediately following the release of the policy details.

Market analysts track sovereign debt buybacks closely because these operations inject liquidity back into financial systems by repurchasing government obligations from holders. The resulting expansion of market liquidity often flows into risk assets and fixed-supply digital commodities. While Bitcoin had experienced periods of consolidation previously, the sovereign debt strategy established a strong structural foundation for the recent market extension, allowing the token to achieve performance levels not observed since the final months of 2024.

## Shifts in Global Regulatory Frameworks

Beyond macroeconomic liquidity drivers, institutional participation in the digital asset sector is being reshaped by regulatory developments in major global financial centers. As published by [Bitcoin Magazine](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/uk-brings-crypto-under-fca-oversight), the United Kingdom has formally initiated steps to bring the industry under full regulatory oversight for the first time.

The British Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) has officially begun accepting authorization applications from digital asset firms ahead of a comprehensive regulatory regime scheduled to take full effect next year. This milestone marks a transition from partial registration requirements toward full compliance supervision. Establishing clear operational guidelines in major markets like the UK gives institutional firms greater certainty, potentially supporting broader market participation alongside macroeconomic tailwinds.

## Broader Market Dynamics and Protocol Research

While Bitcoin led general market momentum over the recent quarter, other segments of the digital asset ecosystem continue to show distinct research developments and token-specific mechanics. Institutional research desks are increasingly analyzing alternative digital assets alongside market benchmarks like Bitcoin and Ethereum.

For instance, research from Standard Chartered highlighted by [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/standard-chartered-says-ethenas-ena-could-crush-bitcoin-and-ethereum-returns-by-2028/) indicates that Ethena's native token, ENA, could potentially outpace the returns of both Bitcoin and Ethereum by 2028. However, current data shows that the supply of Ethena's USDe stablecoin remains below its initial milestone required to trigger revenue-funded token purchases, underscoring the gap between long-term institutional projections and immediate operational metrics.

Concurrently, corporate enterprise testing of public blockchain infrastructure continues to expand globally. As reported by [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/brazils-largest-energy-giant-tests-cardano-to-fix-carbon-double-counting/), Brazil's largest energy company is currently conducting tests on the Cardano blockchain to resolve carbon credit double-counting issues. The platform models aviation fuel environmental benefits as transferable digital tokens that are permanently retired once claimed. While commercial scale for this project remains unquantified, it demonstrates ongoing corporate efforts to leverage distributed ledgers for real-world administrative challenges.

## Understanding the Market Outlook

Bitcoin's 40% quarterly surge reflects the ongoing influence of federal monetary policies on digital asset valuations. The combination of sovereign debt buybacks, shifting regulatory frameworks in key jurisdictions, and continued corporate pilot programs paints a complex picture for the asset class moving into future quarters.

It is important to note that financial markets remain volatile and subject to rapid shifts based on economic data, regulatory changes, and broader market liquidity.
