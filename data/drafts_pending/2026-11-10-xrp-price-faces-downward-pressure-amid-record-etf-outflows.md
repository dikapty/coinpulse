# XRP Price Faces Downward Pressure Amid Record ETF Outflows

As of Tuesday, November 10, 2026, the cryptocurrency market is observing significant volatility surrounding Ripple’s XRP. The asset is currently grappling with substantial downward momentum, reflected in both spot ETF performance and broader price action. Trading at $1.06 on November 10, XRP has experienced a 22% decline in value over the preceding 30 days, creating a challenging environment for market participants.

The recent market activity underscores a cooling interest in institutional products tied to the asset. Data indicates that spot XRP exchange-traded funds (ETFs) have recorded their most significant daily outflows in eight months. This trend of institutional retreat has been further compounded by reports that these financial products have logged zero inflows over a consecutive four-day period leading up to November 10. The lack of new capital entering these vehicles, combined with the scale of the recent withdrawals, has contributed to a bearish outlook among market participants.

## Technical Outlook and Market Sentiment

Financial analysts observing the market have noted that the price of XRP is currently teetering near a potential 10% decline if existing support levels fail to hold. This potential downside is reflective of broader exhaustion within the crypto sector, where even resilient assets are being scrutinized for signs of capitulation. For XRP, the confluence of technical weakness and negative sentiment from institutional investors creates a difficult environment for immediate price recovery.

The 22% loss recorded over the last month highlights the volatility that has defined this period. Investors who entered the market during previous accumulation phases are now facing pressure as the asset struggles to regain its footing. In financial markets, such sharp drawdowns can often lead to a "washout" effect, where discouraged participants exit their positions, which may further accelerate the downward price trajectory. The current situation suggests that market participants are adopting a cautious stance, waiting for clearer signals or improved macroeconomic conditions before re-entering the space. ## Institutional Impact on Crypto Valuations

The performance of spot ETFs serves as a critical barometer for institutional appetite for digital assets. When these products experience a sustained lack of inflows, it often signals a shift in portfolio allocation strategy by large-scale participants. The fact that XRP ETFs have seen zero inflows for four consecutive days suggests that the institutional sector is currently sidelined, or perhaps reallocating capital away from crypto-based instruments in favor of other asset classes. This withdrawal of institutional liquidity can amplify price volatility caused by retail investors, creating a compounding effect on the downside.

Furthermore, the significance of these outflows cannot be overstated. By recording the largest daily withdrawal in eight months, the XRP ETF sector has highlighted a sharp divergence from the growth trends observed earlier in the year. In a market environment where institutional engagement is often viewed as a prerequisite for sustained price increases, the sudden evaporation of that engagement is a notable development. Analysts are currently monitoring the $1.06 price level closely, as it remains a pivot point for determining whether the asset can stabilize or if further technical breakdowns are inevitable.

## Broader Market Context and Implications

It is essential to view the performance of XRP within the context of the broader cryptocurrency market in November 2026. The entire ecosystem is currently dealing with shifting investor behaviors. Recent data has shown that retail investors, who were instrumental in previous market cycles, are also showing signs of capitulation. Specifically, the total number of non-empty Bitcoin (BTC) wallets has fallen by nearly 109,000 since October, suggesting a widespread reduction in retail participation across the board. This exodus of individual investors, combined with the institutional cooling observed in XRP ETFs, points to a period of contraction for the cryptocurrency sector as a whole.

This trend raises questions regarding the sustainability of current price levels across many digital assets. When both institutional and retail participants reduce their exposure simultaneously, the market often experiences heightened volatility and liquidity challenges. For those holding XRP, the combination of technical downside risks and the absence of inflows into ETF products suggests that the coming days will be pivotal. As the market digests these developments, observers are looking for any indication of a bottoming process or a potential reversal in the trend of outflows. Market participants should be aware that cryptocurrency assets are subject to extreme fluctuations, and the current indicators demonstrate a period of significant uncertainty for investors involved in the XRP ecosystem and the broader digital asset space. Any investment strategy should account for the risk of rapid declines in market value.
