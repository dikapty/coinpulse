# Binance hit by Security Breach as Bitcoin Price Drops Below $79,000

A massive security compromise targeting centralized cryptocurrency exchange Binance led to billions of dollars in stolen digital assets and sent broader cryptocurrency prices sliding lower. On October 29, 2026, details of a multi-billion-dollar exploit emerged, revealing that illicit asset transfers triggered widespread market volatility and pushed Bitcoin's price down below the $79,000 mark.

According to an investigation published by blockchain intelligence firm TRM Labs, the attacker engaged in a 36-hour preparation period before executing the heist. The exploit ultimately resulted in $6.36 billion in total losses and involved complex asset movements executed across 10 distinct blockchain networks, standing out as one of the largest security incidents recorded in the digital asset sector.

## Preparation and Execution of the $6.36 Billion Hack

Forensic research from [TRM Labs](https://cryptoslate.com/binance-hack-began-with-a-296-million-xrp-drain-after-36-hour-preparation/) indicates that the attacker began setting up the operation on October 28, 2026. Following 36 hours of strategic preparation, the malicious actor initiated public fund withdrawals by draining $296 million worth of XRP from Binance during the evening of October 28.

Once the initial XRP drain succeeded, the perpetrator rapidly expanded the attack by executing synchronized transfers across 10 separate blockchains. These widespread outflows accumulated to a total exploit size of $6.36 billion, demonstrating significant technical planning and operational capability on the part of the attacker.

## Market Reaction and Stablecoin Interventions

The immediate fallout from the massive security compromise quickly spread to broader market pricing, as reported by [Bitcoin Magazine](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/bitcoin-price-slides-binance). As news of the multi-billion-dollar loss broke across the industry, market sentiment deteriorated, causing Bitcoin to slide under $79,000.

In an attempt to contain the damage and limit the movement of stolen capital, major stablecoin operators intervened. Issuers Tether and Circle took action to restrict wallet addresses tied to the security breach, freezing an estimated $1.8 billion to $2.4 billion in stablecoins connected to the illicit activity.

## On-Chain Intelligence and Mitigation Efforts

Analysis conducted by TRM Labs highlights that the perpetrator established deep access to underlying infrastructure well before moving assets on-chain. The 36-hour pre-attack window suggests deliberate operational staging to ensure maximum extraction speed once transfers commenced.

By splitting and routing the $6.36 billion across 10 separate blockchain networks following the initial $296 million XRP siphon, the hacker sought to hinder real-time tracking efforts. Despite these evasion tactics, rapid cooperation among blockchain analytics teams and stablecoin issuers allowed for the swift freezing of up to $2.4 billion in stablecoins.

## Wider Industry Significance and Infrastructure Vulnerabilities

The scale of capital drained from Binance highlights persistent systemic risks involved in managing centralized exchange hot wallets and cross-chain balances. Security breaches of this magnitude frequently compel trading platforms and infrastructure providers to implement stricter anomaly detection systems capable of identifying unauthorized access during preliminary setup phases.
