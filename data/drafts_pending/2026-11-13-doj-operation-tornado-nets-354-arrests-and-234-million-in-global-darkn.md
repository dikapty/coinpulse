# DOJ Operation TorNado Nets 354 Arrests and $234 Million in Global Darknet Sweep

In one of the largest international law enforcement actions targeting darknet marketplaces and illicit drug distribution networks, the U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) announced significant results from a multi-year global initiative known as Operation TorNado. According to report details published by [Bitcoin Magazine](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/doj-europol-operation-tornado-234-million) on November 12, 2026, the global crackdown led to the arrest of 354 individuals alongside the seizure of $234 million in combined cash and cryptocurrency assets.

The extensive law enforcement operation ran from May 2025 through October 2026, marking a major milestone in cross-border federal law enforcement efforts. The targeted enforcement action specifically focused on vendors, facilitators, and digital payment infrastructure supporting illegal drug trafficking across hidden darknet channels and online marketplaces.

## Unprecedented Scope Across Global Darknet Infrastructure

The Department of Justice noted that Operation TorNado represents the sixth and most extensive operation conducted under the Joint Criminal Opioid and Darknet Enforcement (JCODE) task force framework since its creation in 2018. Over the course of the 18-month initiative, federal agents and international partner agencies coordinated to dismantle supply chains operating across concealed network protocols.

In addition to the financial assets seized during the international sweep, authorities confiscated substantial amounts of physical contraband across multiple participating countries. The final operational tallies published by law enforcement include 5.7 metric tons of illicit drugs and 209 firearms recovered during field actions across participating jurisdictions.

## Digital Currency Tracking and Asset Seizures

Financial assets played a central role in the operation's findings and overall strategy. The total $234 million recovered by international law enforcement was comprised of both physical fiat currency and digital assets. However, in their formal press announcements, federal authorities did not explicitly break down how much of the $234 million total was held in cryptocurrency versus traditional cash.

Digital currencies remain a primary focus for federal task forces such as JCODE due to their frequent use as payment rails on darknet marketplaces. Modern enforcement operations increasingly deploy sophisticated blockchain analytics and digital tracking protocols alongside physical surveillance to trace illicit payments back to physical distribution nodes, bank accounts, and darknet account holders.

## International Law Enforcement Strategy and Institutional Evolution

The overall scale of Operation TorNado highlights the continuing evolution of law enforcement methodologies since JCODE was initially launched in 2018. By combining the investigative capabilities of the DOJ, international bodies such as Europol, and various federal investigative units, participating agencies have expanded their operational capacity to conduct sustained, multi-year enforcement actions across international boundaries.

While darknet marketplaces rely heavily on anonymizing technologies and decentralized infrastructure, operations of this magnitude underscore the persistent vulnerabilities present at the intersection of digital asset transactions, cross-border shipping networks, and local distribution hubs. This record-setting operation marks the continued expansion of JCODE's ongoing international efforts to disrupt digital shadow economies and illicit financial flows.
