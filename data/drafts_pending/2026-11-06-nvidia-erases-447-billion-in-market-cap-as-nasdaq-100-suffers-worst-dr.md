# Nvidia Erases $447 Billion in Market Cap as Nasdaq-100 Suffers Worst Drop Since 2020

On Thursday, November 5, 2026, technology stocks experienced a severe market correction led by semiconductor giant Nvidia (NASDAQ: NVDA). According to reporting by [Finbold](https://finbold.com/nvidia-stock-wipes-447-billion-in-a-day-as-nasdaq-100-suffers-worst-drop-since-2020/), Nvidia saw its market valuation fall by $447 billion in a single day. The sharp drop contributed directly to the Nasdaq-100 index recording its steepest single-day percentage decline since 2020.

The equity downturn came immediately following the recent U.S. midterm elections, which introduced fresh political and macroeconomic uncertainty into domestic markets. High-valuation technology equities, which had previously anchored major market indexes throughout recent rally cycles, bore the brunt of the immediate post-election sell-off as institutional and retail investors reassessed risk exposure across mega-cap growth stocks.

## Record Single-Day Capital Losses Hit Semiconductor Sector

The $447 billion reduction in Nvidia's market capitalization represents one of the largest single-day value erasures for a publicly traded company in financial market history. Because mega-cap technology firms hold heavy weightings in major benchmark indexes, Nvidia's steep decline dragged down broader market benchmarks, impacting supply chain partners, semiconductor peers, and broader risk-asset portfolios.

As reported by [Finbold](https://finbold.com/nvidia-stock-wipes-447-billion-in-a-day-as-nasdaq-100-suffers-worst-drop-since-2020/), the sell-off extended across multiple market sectors, triggering heightened volatility across technology equities. Market analysts noted that the post-midterm trading session reflected rapid sentiment shifts, with high-multiple growth equities experiencing immediate valuation compression under heavy trading volume.

## Market Spillover Affects Broad Technology Equities

The downward momentum was not limited to semiconductor manufacturers. Broad selling pressure impacted equities across adjacent growth and innovative technology sectors during Thursday's trading session. Related reporting by [Finbold](https://finbold.com/sell-alert-for-spacex-stock-as-spcx-targets-crash-to-108/) highlighted severe pressure on tech-adjacent counters, including SpaceX (NASDAQ: SPCX), which declined from $135.92 to $123.62 within 24 hours, with price targets sliding toward $108 amid the broader equity retreat.

The simultaneous pressure across semiconductor manufacturers, component suppliers, and broader growth equities underscores how quickly liquidity receded from high-beta sectors following the election results. As institutional investors realigned portfolios, equity market volatility indexes spiked across major domestic exchanges.

## Evaluating AI Semiconductor Valuations Looking Toward 2027

Following the sharp valuation reset, market participants are scrutinizing the long-term fundamentals and competitive positioning of top artificial intelligence hardware providers. To evaluate strategic trajectories looking toward 2027, [Finbold](https://finbold.com/nvidia-or-amd-we-asked-ai-which-stock-is-better-positioned-for-2027/) conducted an analysis querying two advanced AI models to compare Nvidia against primary semiconductor rival Advanced Micro Devices (AMD).

The comparative analysis highlighted key structural factors influencing both chipmakers as the industry matures. While Nvidia maintains a substantial market share in high-performance AI training accelerators and enterprise data center infrastructure, competitive pressure from AMD's expanding hardware lineup and alternative chip architectures continues to evolve. Investors are weighing whether valuation compression creates entry points or signals longer-term margin normalization across the semiconductor supply chain.

## Institutional Focus Turns to Global Financial Infrastructure

While public stock exchanges manage short-term valuation resets, international financial stakeholders continue to focus on medium-term policy frameworks, regulatory updates, and cross-border investment channels. As reported by [Finbold](https://finbold.com/cyprus-fintech-summit-2026-to-convene-policymakers-investors-and-industry-leaders-from-europe-india-the-gcc-and-africa-in-nicosia/), international finance leaders will gather for the 7th edition of the Cyprus Fintech Summit (CFS) on November 12–13, 2026.

Taking place for the first time in Nicosia, the summit is scheduled to convene policymakers, institutional investors, and fintech executives from Europe, India, the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) region, and Africa. Agenda topics include financial technology infrastructure, cross-border capital flows, and international regulatory alignment. Industry gatherings such as the Cyprus Fintech Summit emphasize ongoing structural investments in global financial plumbing, even as public equity markets navigate periodic sessions of acute volatility.
