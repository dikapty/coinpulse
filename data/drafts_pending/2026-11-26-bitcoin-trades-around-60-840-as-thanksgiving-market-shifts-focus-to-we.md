# Bitcoin Trades Around $60,840 as Thanksgiving Market Shifts Focus to Wealth Metrics

Bitcoin (BTC) experienced a localized relief rally on Thanksgiving, November 26, 2026, pushing its price back above the $60,000 threshold. The primary digital asset registered a 3.8% rebound to trade at approximately $60,840, according to market data reported by [Finbold](https://finbold.com/is-bitcoin-a-buy-before-black-friday/). This short-term upward movement arrives directly ahead of the Black Friday commercial period, offering temporary price relief following prior market contraction.

Despite the immediate gains recorded on November 26, broader network analysis indicates notable shifts in wealth metrics across the blockchain. Data detailed by [Finbold](https://finbold.com/how-many-bitcoin-millionaires-are-there-this-thanksgiving/) reveals that the number of Bitcoin millionaire addresses—defined as unique wallet addresses holding at least $1 million worth of BTC—experienced a sharp decline by Thanksgiving 2026. This contraction highlights the lingering structural impact of recent price pullbacks on high-value network balances.

## Holiday Price Action and Black Friday Dynamics

The 3.8% surge on November 26 lifted Bitcoin to the $60,840 level during holiday trading. As noted by [Finbold](https://finbold.com/is-bitcoin-a-buy-before-black-friday/), market participants are evaluating asset valuations and potential buying opportunities heading into Black Friday. Holiday trading windows frequently display altered trading volumes as institutional activity shifts and retail market interest reacts to short-term price movements.

The push back above $60,000 underscores localized spot demand, though market observers continue to assess whether the movement represents a lasting reversal or a temporary consolidation phase. The performance of Bitcoin during this holiday window remains a key metric for investors tracking digital asset trends prior to the final month of 2026.

## Contraction in High-Value Network Addresses

While short-term spot prices moved higher on Thanksgiving, on-chain metrics point to a reduction in high-tier wallet values over recent months. According to research from [Finbold](https://finbold.com/how-many-bitcoin-millionaires-are-there-this-thanksgiving/), the overall tally of addresses holding $1 million or more in BTC dropped substantially leading up to the holiday.

Because wallet valuations denominated in U.S. dollars track the underlying market price of Bitcoin, macro market downturns pull balances below key monetary thresholds. When Bitcoin experiences price declines, addresses holding amounts slightly above $1 million drop out of the millionaire tier. The lower address count observed by Thanksgiving 2026 directly reflects the valuation pressures present prior to the November 26 price bounce.

## Interpreting On-Chain Address Metrics

On-chain metrics tracking millionaire addresses offer valuable data regarding wealth distribution trends across the network, though analysts highlight important nuances in data interpretation. Public wallet addresses do not correspond directly to individual investors on a one-to-one basis. A single high-net-worth individual or institutional entity may hold assets distributed across multiple distinct public keys.

Conversely, centralized exchanges aggregate the funds of thousands of individual retail customers into large cold-storage addresses. Despite these interpretation limits, significant drops in the count of $1 million-plus addresses serve as a broader indicator of network capital concentration. The contraction reported by [Finbold](https://finbold.com/how-many-bitcoin-millionaires-are-there-this-thanksgiving/) confirms that fewer discrete wallet addresses maintain seven-figure dollar valuations following recent market adjustments.

The dual developments of a Thanksgiving price recovery and contracting high-value address balances occur within a broader market environment of mixed asset expectations. While Bitcoin demonstrated a 3.8% gain to trade at $60,840, altcoins present distinct performance profiles heading into the end of the year.

For context, artificial intelligence model projections evaluating secondary digital assets like XRP indicate muted expectations for a major end-of-year rally, as reported by [Finbold](https://finbold.com/ai-sets-xrp-price-for-the-end-of-2026/).
