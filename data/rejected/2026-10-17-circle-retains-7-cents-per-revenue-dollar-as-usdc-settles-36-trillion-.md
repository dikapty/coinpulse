# Circle Retains 7 Cents Per Revenue Dollar as USDC Settles $36 Trillion in H1

Stablecoin issuer Circle processed more than $36 trillion in transaction volume for its USDC token during the first half of 2026. However, corporate financial disclosures show that the business retained only a small fraction of its top-line earnings due to high distribution costs and substantial operational overhead.

According to financial reporting covered by [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/usdc-processed-36-trillion-in-h1-transactions-but-circle-kept-just-7-cents-of-every-revenue-dollar/), Circle generated roughly $1.4 billion in total revenue over the six-month period. Despite the network settling tens of trillions of dollars in value, the company directed 93% of its revenue toward external partners, operational expenses, and ongoing business investments, leaving it with just 7 cents of net retained revenue for every dollar generated.

## Breakdowns of Distribution Costs and Operating Overhead

The primary driver behind Circle's narrow profit retention is the cost required to support global adoption and maintain liquidity across trading venues. Out of its $1.4 billion in top-line revenue during the first half of 2026, Circle paid out $819 million to distribution partners and transaction-related channels.

Commercial relationships with distribution partners remain essential for stablecoin issuers. Cryptocurrency exchanges, financial technology platforms, and institutional liquidity providers frequently require revenue-sharing agreements, interest payouts, or commercial incentives to integrate, list, and host specific digital assets. Beyond these direct partner distributions, Circle incurred an additional $473 million in operating expenses over the same six-month window, further reducing the net income available to the parent company. ## Massive Network Throughput Meets Narrow Corporate Margins

These financial metrics illustrate a clear divergence between overall network settlement volume and underlying corporate profitability within the stablecoin sector. Handling $36 trillion in transaction volume during H1 2026 underscores widespread adoption of USDC across decentralized finance (DeFi) protocols, cross-border settlement channels, and centralized exchange order books.

However, the requirement to share top-line revenue highlights the competitive landscape stablecoin operators navigate. The $473 million spent on operational costs reflects the continuous resource allocation needed to maintain blockchain infrastructure, manage global regulatory compliance frameworks, and scale administrative functions. As a result, driving higher transaction throughput across a public ledger does not automatically lead to equal profit expansion for the token issuer.

## Long-Term Implications for Reserve-Backed Stablecoin Models

Circle’s revenue allocation across the first six months of 2026 emphasizes the thin-margin dynamics inherent in managing reserve-backed digital dollars. While strong volume figures show robust user demand for the underlying token, the expense distribution confirms that securing distribution channels requires sharing the majority of interest and operational income with third parties.

As competition in the digital asset market persists, industry analysts and market participants will monitor whether stablecoin issuers can adjust their partner commercial terms, lower fixed operational overhead, or achieve economies of scale necessary to improve net revenue retention over time.
