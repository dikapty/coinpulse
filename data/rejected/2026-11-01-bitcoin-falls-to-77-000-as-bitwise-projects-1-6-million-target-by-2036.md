# Bitcoin Drops to $77,000 as Bitwise Projects Long-Term Target of $1.6 Million by 2036

Bitcoin faces heightened price volatility entering November 2026, as market dynamics create a sharp divide between short-term technical stress and long-term institutional price projections. The digital asset experienced a decline into the $77,000 region following a breakdown below key support levels, even as asset manager Bitwise released an updated long-term valuation target for the cryptocurrency.

According to market analysis published by [CryptoPotato](https://cryptopotato.com/bitcoin-price-prediction-for-the-week-will-btc-reclaim-80k-or-dump-toward-70k/), Bitcoin’s drop intensified after buyers failed to hold the $80,000 support zone. Concurrently, reporting from [CryptoPotato](https://cryptopotato.com/bitcoin-to-1-6-million-bitwise-raises-2036-price-target-despite-brutal-market-downturn/) highlights that Bitwise has raised its 2036 Bitcoin price target to $1.6 million per coin, maintaining an optimistic multi-year projection despite a market downturn, slowing exchange-traded fund (ETF) inflows, and gold capturing the majority of store-of-value investments.

## Technical Breakdown Below $80,000 Puts Focus on $70,000 Level

Bitcoin's short-term market structure weakened after sellers pushed prices below the critical $80,000 threshold. On the daily chart, Bitcoin broke beneath an ascending structure that had previously supported its price movement. This breakdown accelerated selling pressure, pulling the asset down into the $77,000 price range.

Market metrics indicate that Bitcoin is currently testing a major daily demand zone. As the asset trades near $77,000, on-chain data shows the price is approaching the average cost basis of recent holders. Falling below the recent holder cost basis often leads to prolonged consolidation or sell-offs as recent buyers face unrealized losses.

Traders and analysts are closely monitoring whether Bitcoin can mount a recovery to reclaim the $80,000 level or if the current momentum will result in a deeper correction toward the $70,000 range, according to technical analysis from [CryptoPotato](https://cryptopotato.com/bitcoin-price-prediction-for-the-week-will-btc-reclaim-80k-or-dump-toward-70k/). A failure to defend the immediate demand zone could open the door to further downside, while a swift reclaim of lost levels would be required to reset the short-term market structure.

## Bitwise Sets 2036 Price Target at $1.6 Million

While short-term technical conditions suggest persistent market risk, institutional asset manager Bitwise has taken an extended view on the asset class. Bitwise updated its long-term outlook, raising its 2036 price target for Bitcoin to $1.6 million per coin, as reported by [CryptoPotato](https://cryptopotato.com/bitcoin-to-1-6-million-bitwise-raises-2036-price-target-despite-brutal-market-downturn/).

The asset manager's model comes at a time when the broader market is grappling with a price contraction. Bitwise’s long-term thesis persists even as investment flows into spot Bitcoin ETFs experience a noticeable deceleration. The slowing ETF demand reflects a cooling period among institutional investors who previously drove capital into the market during earlier expansion phases.

Furthermore, Bitwise acknowledges that traditional assets, particularly gold, continue to capture the vast majority of capital allocated to store-of-value investments. Despite gold maintaining its dominant position among macro investors looking for safe-haven assets, Bitwise's model assumes a multi-year shift that gradually increases Bitcoin’s share of the store-of-value market over the next decade.

## Macro Dynamics and ETF Inflow Slowdown

The divergence between current market performance and long-term forecasts underscores the macro environment facing digital assets. In recent months, traditional store-of-value assets like gold have attracted capital allocations, benefiting from broader economic uncertainties and investor risk aversion. This trend has limited capital rotation into higher-risk digital assets like Bitcoin.

Additionally, the slowdown in spot Bitcoin ETF flows highlights changing institutional behavior. The initial wave of institutional adoption following ETF launches provided a significant tailwind for crypto asset prices. However, with net inflows easing, the market has become more reliant on spot market dynamics, which have struggled to hold key technical support levels like $80,000.

Bitwise's $1.6 million target relies on the premise that structural adoption and supply dynamics will outpace current cyclical downturns over a ten-year horizon. However, achieving such valuations remains contingent on Bitcoin expanding its role as a competing store-of-value asset against gold.

## Short-Term Support Levels and Market Outlook

In the immediate term, market participants remain focused on Bitcoin's interaction with major technical support zones. The daily chart structure shows that a sustained breach of the $77,000 region could accelerate liquidations, driving the asset toward the psychological $70,000 level.

Conversely, if buyers can defend the current daily demand zone and absorb selling pressure from recent holders trading near their cost basis, a technical rebound could attempt to retest the $80,000 resistance. Reclaiming $80,000 is seen by market analysts as essential for invalidating the immediate bearish momentum on daily chart timeframes.

As the market navigates November 2026, the contrast between short-term technical weakness and multi-year projections highlights the dual narratives currently driving the cryptocurrency space. Investors continue to evaluate immediate downside technical risks against long-term structural forecasts.
