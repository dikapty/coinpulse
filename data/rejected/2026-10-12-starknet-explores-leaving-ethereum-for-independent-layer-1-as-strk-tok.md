# Starknet Explores Leaving Ethereum for Independent Layer 1 as STRK Token Rallies

Starknet is actively considering an architectural pivot that would see it exit its position as an Ethereum Layer 2 scaling solution to build an independent, quantum-resistant Layer 1 blockchain. The potential strategy shift, announced by the project on October 8, 2026, has ignited a sharp increase in trading activity and driven double-digit price gains for its native asset, STRK.

According to market data captured via a CoinGlass snapshot at 20:28 UTC on October 11, 2026, the STRK token reached $0.1190. This price point represented a 17.7% advance over a 24-hour period and a 116.7% rise over seven days, effectively more than doubling the token's valuation within a week as market participants digested the network's potential structural changes.

## Starknet Considers Shift to Quantum-Resistant Layer 1

The central driver behind the market movement is Starknet's ongoing evaluation of its relationship with the Ethereum mainnet. On October 8, 2026, the project revealed that it was actively considering becoming an independent Layer 1 network. Under the proposal being weighed by developers, the project would shift away from its existing role as an Ethereum Layer 2 network, where it currently relies on Ethereum for base-layer consensus and final settlement.

A primary motivation outlined for the potential transition is the deployment of a native layer featuring explicit quantum resistance. As cryptographic advancement poses long-term security considerations for existing blockchain infrastructures, Starknet’s team is evaluating whether establishing a standalone Layer 1 blockchain provides a superior architecture to implement quantum-resistant security parameters directly at the protocol layer, rather than operating within the execution constraints of Ethereum's base layer.

## Market Impact and STRK Trading Activity

The announcement on October 8 sparked significant market engagement around the STRK token. Over the seven-day period leading into October 11, trading activity surged alongside the project's public deliberation, culminating in what market data tracked as a multi-billion-dollar trading frenzy surrounding the asset.

The price trajectory recorded by CoinGlass highlights the intensity of the market response. Sitting at $0.1190 late on October 11, STRK's 116.7% seven-day gain reflects a rapid reassessment of the token by market participants following the proposal. The 17.7% 24-hour gain noted at 20:28 UTC on October 11 indicates that buying momentum remained active days after the initial October 8 disclosure.

## Strategic Implications of an Ethereum Departure

Starknet’s deliberation over leaving Ethereum represents a notable development within the broader Layer 2 and scaling ecosystem. Historically, Layer 2 networks have built their value propositions around inheriting the underlying security and liquidity of Ethereum while offering lower transaction fees and higher throughput. Choosing to decouple from Ethereum to establish an independent Layer 1 would require Starknet to recruit and secure its own set of validators or consensus mechanisms to maintain network integrity.

Transitioning to a standalone Layer 1 would also mean Starknet must manage its own security model without relying on Ethereum for finality. While this path presents technical and operational hurdles, the focus on building a quantum-resistant network suggests developers are prioritizing custom base-layer features that may be difficult to implement while tied directly to Ethereum’s current execution rules. How Starknet structures consensus, validator incentives, and cross-chain connectivity will be critical factors if the team decides to proceed with the transition.

## Next Steps for Developers and Token Holders

As of October 12, 2026, the transition remains under active consideration by Starknet developers. The project has not finalized the operational timeline or the exact technical specifications required to execute a full migration from Layer 2 to an independent Layer 1.

For token holders and ecosystem users, the ongoing deliberations mean that Starknet's long-term technical roadmap, security parameters, and network architecture remain subject to future protocol governance and developer decisions. Market participants will likely continue monitoring official developer channels for updates regarding network design, migration mechanisms, and validator structure.
