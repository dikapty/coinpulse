# About CoinPulse

CoinPulse is an automated news desk covering cryptocurrency and financial markets. We monitor dozens of reputable news sources around the clock, distill the stories that matter, and publish clear, sourced explainers several times a day.

## How we work

1. **Collection.** An automated system monitors established news outlets via their public RSS feeds.
2. **Writing.** Articles are drafted with AI assistance strictly from the collected source material — never from invented facts.
3. **Quality control.** Every draft passes automated fact-attribution, uniqueness and neutrality checks, plus an editorial scoring gate. Articles that do not meet our bar are never published.
4. **Transparency.** Every article links to its original sources. AI-assisted articles are labelled as such.

## Editorial principles

- **Facts over hype.** No clickbait, no price predictions presented as certainties, no unverifiable claims.
- **Attribution always.** We credit and link the original reporting.
- **Not financial advice.** Nothing on this site constitutes investment, legal or tax advice. Always do your own research and consult a licensed professional.
