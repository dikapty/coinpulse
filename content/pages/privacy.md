# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: see the site footer / repository history._

## What we collect

CoinPulse is a static website. We do not require accounts and do not collect personal data directly.

## Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics (for example Google Analytics 4 or a self-hosted counter) to understand aggregate traffic — pages viewed, referrers, country. This data is not used to identify individual visitors.

## Cookies and advertising

When advertising is enabled on this site, third-party vendors (such as Google AdSense) may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).

## Affiliate links

Some articles contain affiliate links, always clearly labelled. If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This never influences our editorial coverage.

## Third-party content

Articles link to external news sources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

## Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR, CCPA, etc.) you may have rights regarding your personal data. Since we do not collect personal data directly, requests generally concern analytics or advertising providers — see their respective policies or contact us via the channel in the repository README.
