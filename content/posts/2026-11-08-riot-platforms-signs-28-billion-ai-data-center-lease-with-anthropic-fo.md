# Riot Platforms Signs $28 Billion AI Data Center Lease With Anthropic for Texas Site

Bitcoin miner Riot Platforms has secured a $28 billion lease agreement with artificial intelligence startup Anthropic, marking a major convergence between cryptocurrency mining infrastructure and enterprise AI computing demands. Under the binding contract, Anthropic will lease capacity at Riot's data center facility located in Corsicana, Texas.

The deal reflects a growing trend among digital asset mining companies expanding or repurposing their balance sheets and infrastructure to accommodate high-density AI workloads. According to reporting by [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/riots-28-billion-ai-lease-gives-anthropic-a-veto-on-voluntary-power-cuts/), the binding lease agreement grants Anthropic specific control over facility power operations, highlighting the specialized operational terms required when hosting continuous high-performance computing.

## Terms of the Corsicana Power Agreement

The binding lease covers a planned capacity of 910 megawatts (MW) at the Corsicana, Texas site. Under the structure of the contract, rent payments are directly tied to future delivery, ensuring that financial obligations correspond to power availability and operational scale over time.

A central provision of the contract grants Anthropic a veto right over voluntary power cuts at the Texas facility. This clause ensures the AI firm can maintain continuous uptime during normal operating conditions, preventing discretionary grid participation from interrupting intensive computational processing and model operations.

However, the contract explicitly differentiates voluntary curtailment from regulatory compliance. Mandatory grid orders remain strictly exempt from Anthropic’s veto rights, ensuring that the facility continues to comply with state and regional power grid operators during emergency conditions or required grid stabilization events.

## Shifting Dynamics in Power Load Management

The inclusion of a veto on voluntary power cutbacks marks a distinct shift from standard crypto mining operational practices. Historically, flexible load management has served as an important component of Bitcoin mining business models. Mining firms frequently participate in demand-response programs, voluntarily reducing power usage during peak grid demand hours to earn operational incentives or secure lower overall energy rates.

By granting Anthropic a veto over voluntary cutbacks, Riot prioritizes uninterrupted operational stability over flexible curtailment revenue for the leased 910 MW capacity block. Artificial intelligence operations require continuous power delivery and cannot be interrupted as easily as Bitcoin mining rigs without risking operational disruptions and commercial losses.

At the same time, keeping mandatory grid orders exempt from the veto ensures that regulatory compliance remains intact. The Corsicana facility must still respond when grid operators issue mandatory curtailment orders to protect power grid stability.

## Convergence of Crypto Infrastructure and AI Capital

Riot's allocation of substantial electrical capacity to AI workloads occurs alongside broader institutional movements bridging digital asset infrastructure and emerging technologies. Venture capital firms and technology platforms are increasingly expanding their focus across both sectors to capture high-density computing demand.

This trend is reflected across recent venture capital shifts. For example, venture capital giant Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) broadened its investment mandate beyond traditional crypto-native projects to include artificial intelligence and other emerging technologies, as reported by [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/10/22/a16z-is-the-latest-vc-giant-to-widen-bets-beyond-crypto-and-into-ai-and-emerging-tech).

Concurrently, early-stage capital is funding decentralized infrastructure designed specifically to support AI ecosystems. In October 2026, startup Dataline raised $10.6 million to build a decentralized data network for AI agents, as detailed by [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/deals/2026-10-21-dataline-decentralized-data-network-ai-agents-funding-ico-421596). Dataline also announced plans to conduct a public token sale using Uniswap's auction platform.

## Enterprise Integration and Long-Term Revenue

For digital asset miners, long-term leasing agreements provide predictable, recurring revenue streams that mitigate the volatility associated with cryptocurrency network difficulty changes and block reward fluctuations. Securing a binding $28 billion lease establishes a multi-year cash flow anchor linked directly to enterprise AI compute demand.

This integration coincides with wider efforts to connect digital asset tools into standard corporate balance sheets. As reported by [CryptoPotato](https://cryptopotato.com/ripple-xrp-just-made-a-major-move-into-corporate-finance/), enterprise crypto products are expanding into traditional corporate finance, including initiatives from Ripple that enable eligible businesses to access yield opportunities on qualifying digital assets, such as RLUSD, outside standard banking hours.

Together, these developments highlight how physical energy assets, digital asset infrastructure, and artificial intelligence compute requirements are increasingly overlapping across the technology and financial sectors.
