# Super League Stockholders Approve Metaplanet Investment Structure to Enable 2,100 BTC Strategy

Stockholders of Nasdaq-listed Super League Enterprise, Inc. have officially approved an investment structure with Japanese firm Metaplanet Inc., clearing a critical hurdle for a transaction that integrates a significant Bitcoin reserve strategy into the corporate entity. According to a report by [Bitcoin Magazine](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/metaplanet-super-league-deal-approved), the approved framework paves the way for Metaplanet to acquire an equity stake of approximately 96% in Super League, leading to a rebranded corporate combination referred to as Superplanet.

Under the terms presented to equity holders, the corporate arrangement incorporates a framework to execute a 2,100 Bitcoin (BTC) treasury strategy. The voting results mark an important milestone for both companies, enabling Tokyo-listed Metaplanet to expand its balance sheet approach into U.S. public markets while granting Super League a direct shift toward cryptocurrency reserve management.

## Transaction Details and the Transition to Superplanet

The approved transaction allows Tokyo-based Metaplanet to secure a controlling stake of roughly 96% in Super League, effectively restructuring the company's capital setup. The strategic transformation, which includes renaming the combined operations to Superplanet following the shareholder vote, reflects Metaplanet's established policy of using Bitcoin as a primary corporate treasury reserve asset.

By securing voting control over a Nasdaq-listed entity, Metaplanet establishes an operational platform within North American capital markets. The approval authorizes the execution of the 2,100 BTC strategy, embedding digital asset reserves directly into the corporate structure of the business.

This balance sheet integration relies on holding actual Bitcoin rather than derivative financial products, creating an operational setup where the combined public entity operates with explicit cryptocurrency allocations. Super League shareholders evaluated and endorsed the proposal during a special meeting to facilitate this strategic shift in corporate direction.

## Stockholder Voting Results and Meeting Breakdown

According to official disclosures cited by [Bitcoin Magazine](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/metaplanet-super-league-deal-approved), the special meeting of stockholders yielded substantial support for all transaction-related proposals required to move the deal forward. Super League shareholders cast votes on key structural amendments, including an increase in authorized common stock and the issuance of shares to Metaplanet.

Specifically, Proposal 1—which authorized the share issuance giving Metaplanet its majority position—received overwhelming approval, with 12,450,110 votes in favor, 185,210 votes against, and 12,050 abstentions. Proposal 2, concerning the expansion of authorized common stock necessary to accommodate the new capital setup, passed with 12,465,300 votes in favor, 172,010 votes against, and 10,060 abstentions.

Additional votes were held on corporate governance adjustments and name-change provisions designed to complete the rebranding to Superplanet. Proposal 3 passed with 12,440,500 votes in favor and 190,020 against, while Proposal 4 passed with 12,470,100 votes in favor and 165,000 against. These voting margins provided the necessary corporate authorization to proceed toward final execution.

## Treasury Strategies and Public Market Expansion

Metaplanet's acquisition strategy aligns with a growing global trend of corporate entities adopting dedicated Bitcoin reserve policies to manage treasury assets and capital structures. Already recognized for accumulating substantial Bitcoin holdings on its balance sheet in Japan, Metaplanet's latest move provides direct exposure to U.S. equity market investors through Super League's Nasdaq platform.

For public market investors, corporate structures that maintain dedicated cryptocurrency reserves offer an alternative stock-based holding vehicle. By integrating a 2,100 BTC allocation target directly into the corporate strategy of Superplanet, the combined entity aims to leverage equity market liquidity to build and maintain its digital asset treasury.
