# Bitcoin Rainbow Chart Projects Wide Valuation Range for December 2026

As of November 11, 2026, cryptocurrency market participants are evaluating long-term valuation metrics for Bitcoin (BTC) as technical models present broad possibilities for the asset's trajectory heading into the final month of the year. According to updated data from the Bitcoin Rainbow Chart, Bitcoin could trade within a wide theoretical price channel ranging from $114,614 on the lower bound to $1,346,386 on the upper bound by December 1, 2026, as reported by [Finbold](https://finbold.com/bitcoin-rainbow-chart-predicts-btc-price-for-december-1-2026/).

This extensive price interval underscores the inherent volatility and long-term logarithmic growth assumptions embedded in historical Bitcoin forecasting models. While the chart provides a structured visual framework based on past market cycles, the wide spread between the minimum and maximum projections reflects the significant uncertainty surrounding macro market conditions, liquidity shifts, and digital asset adoption rates.

## Understanding the Bitcoin Rainbow Chart Framework

The Bitcoin Rainbow Chart is a long-term valuation tool designed to plot BTC price performance on a logarithmic growth curve. By using color-coded bands, the chart attempts to contextualize current market prices relative to historical market cycles, identifying potential zones of market sentiment ranging from accumulation phases to market tops.

Under the projections evaluated on November 11, 2026, the model outlines an extreme spread exceeding $1.2 million between its lowest and highest bands for December 1, 2026. The lower target of $114,614 represents a scenario consistent with historical floor levels or conservative growth bands within the logarithmic channel. Conversely, the upper target of $1,346,386 reflects an aggressive market cycle peak, characteristic of historical parabolic bull runs mapped onto the upper boundaries of the logarithmic curve.

Market models like the Rainbow Chart are typically used by analysts to evaluate macro trends rather than to execute short-term trades. Because the bands expand over time to accommodate Bitcoin's historical price appreciation, the model inherently produces wider dollar ranges as time horizons extend forward. The utility of the chart lies in its ability to offer a visual representation of historical market behavior, though it does not account for unforeseen geopolitical or economic shocks that might alter the course of the cryptocurrency market.
