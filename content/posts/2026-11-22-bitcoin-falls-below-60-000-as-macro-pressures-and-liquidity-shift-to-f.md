# Bitcoin Falls Below $60,000 as Macro Pressures and Liquidity Shift to Fed's December Meeting

Bitcoin broke below the key $60,000 threshold on Friday, November 20, 2026, marking its lowest price point since July 2026. According to reporting by [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoin-below-60000-fed-half-point-cpi/), the downturn coincided with net withdrawals from US spot Bitcoin funds as macro conditions shifted across traditional asset classes.

Investors are increasingly weighing digital asset exposure against low-risk alternatives in traditional finance. With short-term Treasury bills offering yields of more than 4% per year, institutional capital has faced higher opportunity costs for holding volatile assets. This capital rotation comes at a crucial moment for monetary policy, placing intense focus on the Federal Reserve's upcoming interest rate decision scheduled for December 2026.

## Federal Reserve Policy and Inflation Concerns

The drop below $60,000 complicates the outlook for the Federal Reserve's final policy meeting of the year. Financial market participants are currently anticipating a quarter-point (25 basis point) interest rate increase at the December gathering, based on data reported by [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoin-below-60000-fed-half-point-cpi/).

However, monetary policy perspectives within the central bank remain divided. Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller has publicly emphasized that persistent inflation continues to present an ongoing challenge to economic stability. The tension between managing elevated inflation metrics and monitoring tightening financial conditions leaves asset markets sensitive to upcoming macroeconomic updates. Investors in both traditional equity and digital asset sectors are attempting to price in whether persistent price pressures might force a tighter monetary stance than previously expected.

## Shift in Derivatives Positioning and Market Sentiment

Prior to the price breakdown, the crypto derivatives landscape experienced a massive unwinding of bearish leverage. Over a five-week stretch preceding the recent downturn, reported Bitcoin short contracts dropped by 79%, driven largely by a decline of 3,889 reported short positions, according to analysis from [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoin-shorts-collapsed-79-in-five-weeks-then-the-market-turned-lower/).

Despite this dramatic reduction in short open interest—which typically signals reduced selling pressure—the market was unable to maintain its upward momentum and subsequently turned lower. Derivatives traders and institutional market participants are watching regulatory filings closely to assess how positions are adjusting following the drop. The next official report from the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) is scheduled for release on November 30, 2026, which will provide updated insight into institutional positioning across US futures markets.

## Geopolitical Risks and Derivatives Exchange Outages

Beyond domestic monetary policy, global macroeconomic and infrastructure factors are exerting additional stress on digital asset markets. Recent geopolitical tension in the Middle East has introduced volatility into energy commodities. As detailed by [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/two-hormuz-tanker-strikes-threaten-a-potential-source-of-relief-for-bitcoin/), two reported oil tanker strikes have put a recent decline in Brent crude oil prices to the test. Traders are closely tracking outbound cargo flows alongside US rate expectations, as sudden shifts in energy prices can alter broader inflation forecasts and risk-asset appetite.

At the same time, operational friction affected crypto derivatives trading infrastructure. Market data from [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/deribit-outage-hit-bitcoin-hedgers-on-coinbase/) shows that two trading interruptions impacted global Bitcoin and Ether perpetual contracts that share execution on the Deribit platform. The outages directly affected traders attempting to hedge positions, including those active on Coinbase, though Coinbase clarified that its standalone US derivatives services remained fully operational throughout the events.

## Market Context and Broader Implications

The convergence of structural liquidations, exchange disruptions, geopolitical tensions, and competing yield opportunities underscores the evolving landscape for digital assets as of November 22, 2026.
