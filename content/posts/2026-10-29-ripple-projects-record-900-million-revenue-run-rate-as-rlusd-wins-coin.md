# Ripple Projects Record $900 Million Revenue Run Rate as RLUSD Wins Coinbase Support and Moody's Rating

Blockchain infrastructure provider Ripple is charting a divergent course from its associated digital asset XRP, announcing record-breaking operational figures even as the cryptocurrency trades near multi-year relative lows. On October 28, 2026, the company disclosed that it is on track to exit the year with an annualized product revenue run rate of $900 million. If achieved, the figure will mark the strongest operational business year in Ripple's corporate history.

The commercial growth reflects accelerating adoption of Ripple’s enterprise technology suite, supported by expanding institutional partnerships and rising global market demand. However, the corporate milestone arrives during a complex period for the broader Ripple ecosystem. While corporate balance sheets reflect substantial commercial expansion, the underlying XRP token is simultaneously pacing toward its worst annual performance since 2022. This performance gap underscores an evolving dynamic between corporate technology adoption and public token valuation across the digital asset sector. Investors evaluating these trends should note that this analysis is provided for informational context, and this is not financial advice.

## Institutional Expansion Drives Corporate Business Metrics

The fundamental driver behind Ripple’s projected $900 million revenue run rate is a significant surge in corporate and institutional integration. Throughout 2026, institutional counterparties have increasingly leveraged Ripple’s network architecture for cross-border treasury operations, payment settlements, and liquidity management.

According to data provided by [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/ripple-is-heading-for-its-best-year-ever-while-xrp-faces-its-worst-since-2022/), the firm's commercial operations have scaled rapidly, insulated from the immediate spot market volatility of individual tokens. By focusing on enterprise software distribution, corporate infrastructure integrations, and institutional transaction fees, Ripple has built recurring revenue streams that continue to expand regardless of retail trading sentiment.

This commercial decoupling demonstrates how enterprise blockchain providers can capture operational value even during prolonged downturns in public token markets. While trading volumes across major liquid tokens have faced macroeconomic pressures throughout 2026, institutional demand for underlying ledger mechanisms and standardized liquidity protocols has remained resilient.

## RLUSD Receives Moody's Investment-Grade Rating

A pivotal component of Ripple's institutional strategy culminated on Wednesday, October 28, 2026, with major regulatory and credit milestones for its US dollar-backed stablecoin, RLUSD. Credit rating agency Moody's assigned RLUSD an official investment-grade credit rating, representing a significant external endorsement of the stablecoin's reserve backing and operational framework.

Investment-grade ratings from established tradFi rating firms remain exceedingly rare in the digital asset market. Moody's assessment evaluates the creditworthiness, capital management, and structural safety of the underlying reserves backing the RLUSD token. The rating is designed to provide institutional risk committees and corporate treasurers with formal credit assurances, lowering the risk barriers typically associated with integrated crypto assets.

The credit validation comes as global institutions demand higher transparency and strict collateral management standards from fiat-backed digital instruments. By securing an investment-grade rating, Ripple positions RLUSD as a primary competitor against established market operators, directly targeting institutional treasury departments seeking secure, risk-assessed settlement rails.

## Coinbase and Base Blockchain Integration Expands Distribution

Following the Moody's credit evaluation, Ripple secured a major distribution milestone through a commercial agreement with Coinbase. As reported by [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/ripples-rlusd-stablecoin-gains-coinbase-access-as-moodys-awards-investment-grade-rating/), RLUSD officially launched on Coinbase's trading ecosystem and its native Layer-2 network, Base.

The integration makes RLUSD directly accessible across both Coinbase's retail trading application and its specialized institutional execution platforms. By integrating directly into Base—one of the fastest-growing Ethereum Layer-2 networks—RLUSD gains immediate accessibility within decentralized finance (DeFi) protocols, institutional clearing pathways, and consumer-facing applications.

Access to Coinbase's distribution network provides Ripple with direct exposure to millions of registered accounts and hundreds of institutional market participants in the United States. Expanding distribution channels through premier domestic exchanges is widely viewed as a critical driver for stablecoin liquidity, as circulation velocity depends heavily on seamless fiat on-ramps and multi-venue settlement capabilities.

## Market Contrast: Corporate Growth versus Token Performance

Despite the significant operational milestones achieved by Ripple and its stablecoin initiatives, market conditions for the XRP token present a starkly different narrative. XRP is currently heading toward its weakest annual price performance since 2022, highlighting a persistent disconnect between corporate software revenues and utility token pricing.

Market analysts note that while software licensing, transaction rails, and stablecoin minting generate reliable corporate revenue run rates for Ripple as a company, the spot market price of XRP remains sensitive to broader crypto liquidity cycles, global regulatory shifts, and wider market risk appetite. The launch of RLUSD adds an intriguing variable to this equation, as institutional clients gain access to a dollar-pegged settlement asset alongside XRP's volatile spot liquidity.

As Ripple approaches the close of 2026 with a $900 million revenue trajectory, industry observers will closely track whether expanding stablecoin settlement volume and institutional software usage eventually yield positive liquidity spillovers for the broader XRP ecosystem, or if corporate revenue growth will continue to diverge from underlying asset valuations.
