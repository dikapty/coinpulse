# MEXC Reports Substantial Growth in Tokenized Asset Trading Amid AI Infrastructure Demand

On Thursday, October 29, 2026, the digital asset exchange MEXC released performance data regarding the traditional finance (TradFi) sector on its platform for the month of September 2026. The report details a significant shift in investor interest toward tokenized versions of U.S. stocks and exchange-traded funds (ETFs) that maintain exposure to artificial intelligence. According to the exchange, trading volume for these AI-related tokenized U.S. assets surged by 88% throughout the month of September [MEXC Reports 88% Growth in AI-Related Tokenized U.S. Stock and ETF Trading Volume in September, With Four Infrastructure Sectors Up Over 90%](https://finbold.com/mexc-reports-88-growth-in-ai-related-tokenized-u-s-stock-and-etf-trading-volume-in-september-with-four-infrastructure-sectors-up-over-90/).

This trend highlights a growing integration between traditional equity markets and blockchain-based trading environments. By offering tokenized assets, MEXC provides a platform for users to trade fractions of equities or ETFs. These tokenized products often operate under a zero-fee model on the platform, distinguishing them from the fee structures typically found in traditional brokerage environments. The concentrated interest in AI-linked assets suggests that market participants are utilizing the speed and digital-native accessibility of crypto exchanges to capture growth within the artificial intelligence industry.

## Growth Patterns Within AI Infrastructure

The data provided by MEXC indicates that the surge in trading volume was not uniform across all technology sub-sectors. While the broader category of AI-linked tokenized products saw an 88% increase, four specific infrastructure sectors associated with the AI supply chain experienced growth exceeding 90%. 

This outperformance of specific infrastructure segments points to a granular investor focus on the foundational layers of the AI industry. In the context of market infrastructure, this typically includes the physical and software-based systems necessary to support large-scale machine learning and complex data processing, such as semiconductor manufacturing, cloud computing services, and energy-intensive data center operations. 

For market participants, this growth trajectory provides insight into current capital flows within the digital asset ecosystem. The move toward trading tokenized U.S. equities on centralized crypto exchanges signals an evolution in platform utility, as these venues move beyond native crypto-assets to offer diversified exposure to traditional indices. This is significant because it highlights a preference among a segment of users to consolidate their trading activities into a single digital interface, rather than navigating disparate systems for crypto and traditional stock market participation. ## The Broader Context of Asset Tokenization

Tokenization represents a method of recording ownership rights of an asset on a blockchain. A primary advantage of this process is the ability to facilitate trading cycles that are not confined to the regulated market hours of major traditional stock exchanges in the United States. As observed in the September data, the ability for users to engage with tokenized versions of U.S. stocks allows for a high degree of trading flexibility, a factor that likely contributed to the volume increase reported by the exchange.

The 88% rise in volume throughout September suggests that investors are leveraging these digital tools to capitalize on market activity within the AI sector, regardless of traditional opening and closing bells. Furthermore, the growth in these specific asset classes underscores the ongoing convergence between traditional finance and decentralized finance principles. 

As exchanges continue to expand their TradFi offerings, the competitive landscape for retail trading is shifting. Traditional brokerages and crypto exchanges are increasingly vying for the same demographic of tech-savvy traders who value low friction and rapid execution. By focusing on high-growth areas like AI infrastructure, exchanges are effectively targeting users seeking exposure to the most active sectors of the global economy through the medium of digital tokens.

## Implications for Digital Asset Markets

While the September report focuses on AI infrastructure, it serves as a snapshot of the potential for the broader tokenized asset market. Rapid volume growth often facilitates increased liquidity, which can lead to tighter spreads and more efficient execution for platform users. However, this growth also necessitates ongoing attention to regulatory compliance and the underlying collateralization of tokenized assets. As investors allocate capital to these products, the platform's role in maintaining a transparent and secure backing for the tokenized stocks becomes a critical factor for market stability. 

The success of these products moving forward will likely depend on the platform's ability to maintain a reliable bridge between traditional financial institutions and the digital ledger. The 90% growth in core AI infrastructure categories highlights an increasingly sophisticated investor base, which is moving away from generalized tech exposure toward specific segments driving current economic trends. 

Whether this momentum will sustain through the final quarter of 2026 remains to be seen, but the September data confirms that tokenized TradFi products are currently a significant driver of engagement within the digital asset exchange environment. Market observers will continue to monitor these volume metrics as a barometer for both retail sentiment and the broader integration of traditional equity products into the crypto-asset space.
