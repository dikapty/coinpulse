# Bitget Restores Services Following Major $388 Million Security Breach

As of Monday, October 19, 2026, the cryptocurrency exchange Bitget is in the process of stabilizing its operations following a significant security compromise that occurred in late September 2026. The exchange reported a breach resulting in the loss of approximately $388 million, an event that forced a temporary suspension of various withdrawal services to protect user assets and assess the extent of the damage [Bitget resumes Bitcoin withdrawals as hacker swaps ETH via THORChain](https://cointelegraph.com/news/bitget-btc-withdrawal-hacker-eth-swap-thorchain?utm_source=rss_feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=rss_partner_inbound).

Following the incident, which took place in the days leading up to September 28, 2026, Bitget initiated a phased restoration of its platform's functionality. By September 28, the exchange had successfully resumed Bitcoin withdrawals. This recovery effort was part of a structured plan to re-enable services for various digital assets. According to the timeline provided by the exchange, Ether (ETH) withdrawals were slated for restoration on September 29, 2026, while USDt withdrawals were scheduled to follow on September 30, 2026. This is not financial advice.

## Tracking the Stolen Assets and Technical Hurdles

Security analysts and market observers have closely monitored the movement of the funds stolen during the $388 million exploit. Investigations into the breach revealed that the perpetrator actively utilized decentralized finance (DeFi) infrastructure to obscure the trail of the stolen assets. Specifically, reports identified that the hacker engaged in swapping Ether through the THORChain protocol. The use of such cross-chain liquidity networks often complicates efforts to freeze or recover stolen funds, as these protocols allow for the rapid exchange of assets across different blockchain networks, potentially distancing the stolen tokens from their original source.

For platform users and the broader crypto market, the incident highlights the ongoing challenges exchanges face regarding security and the prevention of large-scale exploits. Bitget’s communication to its user base focused on transparency regarding the service restoration timeline, attempting to mitigate the impact of the outage on liquidity and trading activity. The speed at which an exchange restores withdrawals after an event of this magnitude is a critical factor in maintaining long-term user trust. As of mid-October, the industry remains focused on the exchange’s security posture and the efficacy of its post-incident remediation strategies.

## Implications for Exchange Security and Market Trust

The impact of the Bitget breach extends beyond the immediate loss of funds, serving as a reminder of the persistent security risks inherent in centralized cryptocurrency platforms. When a major exchange suffers a breach of this size, it necessitates a period of intense scrutiny from users. The necessity of halting withdrawals underscores the trade-off between absolute platform security and the fluid access users expect from their chosen exchange. When services are paused, liquidity is trapped, creating a period of uncertainty for those who rely on the platform for their trading activities.

Furthermore, the breach highlights the sophisticated tactics utilized by bad actors in the current landscape. By leveraging protocols like THORChain, attackers can effectively move assets in ways that traditional centralized monitoring tools may struggle to track. This creates a difficult environment for exchanges that are tasked with maintaining high-speed trading while simultaneously implementing rigorous security measures to detect and prevent unauthorized large-scale withdrawals. For the average investor, this serves as a technical reminder to be aware of the custodial risks associated with keeping large portions of a portfolio on centralized exchanges, especially in an era where cybersecurity threats are becoming increasingly complex.
